# Code of conduct

Turnkeeper's public community should be safe, rigorous, and useful to people with different levels
of experience. Participation in this repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
official community spaces requires the behavior below.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Make room for questions and different technical backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Stop harmful behavior when a maintainer asks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, customer data, exploit details, or private company material
- Using community spaces for spam or unrelated promotion
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@turnkeeper.ai`. Include only the information needed to
understand the incident. Do not include credentials, customer content, or unrelated private data.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical. Depending on severity and
history, responses may include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits,
or a permanent ban from project spaces. Immediate restrictions may be applied when needed to protect
people or systems.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report. Retaliation against
reporters or participants in an investigation is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in official Turnkeeper community spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project. It does not replace the separate private vulnerability process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
